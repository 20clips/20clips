FAQ

Can you pull Shorts from my existing long-form YouTube videos?

Yes, this is one of the most common requests. Send the long-form file and we'll find and edit the strongest moments into Shorts.

How many Shorts can come from one long-form video?

It depends on length and content, but a single podcast episode or long-form video often produces several of your 20 monthly videos.

Do you optimize titles and descriptions for Shorts?

We focus on the video edit itself. Titles, descriptions and publishing are handled on your end.

Can the same edit be reused for Reels and TikTok?

Often yes, with minor adjustments to captions and pacing, which we handle as part of the monthly service.
