How remote editing fits different working patterns

A business in the Gulf may film in the evening and review the next morning. A team in the UK may upload straight after a shoot day. An owner in a regional Australian town may film between jobs and review edits once the day is done. A shared folder and a review step suit all three, because nobody has to be in the same room or even awake at the same time.

The same logic applies to creators. Whether you record a podcast in a spare room or a studio, you send the recording and the clips return. For ideas on how many videos suit your channel, read how many short-form videos to post each week.
