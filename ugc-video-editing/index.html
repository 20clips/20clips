FAQ

Is UGC editing only for paid ads, or also organic content?

Both. The same raw UGC footage is often edited into organic short-form videos and paid ad creatives.

Do you produce multiple hook variations from one piece of UGC?

Yes, hook and opening variations are a standard part of UGC and performance creative editing.

Do you find or manage UGC creators for us?

No, we edit footage you already have. Sourcing and briefing UGC creators is managed on your end.

Can UGC edits be used directly in Meta or TikTok ad accounts?

Yes, videos are delivered in formats suitable for uploading directly to ad platforms.
