Sydney is image-conscious, and its owners are busy

Sydney's businesses and creators compete in a crowded, image-conscious market. Property agents, hospitality groups, fitness and wellness studios, fashion and lifestyle brands, tradies, coaches and personal brands all use short-form video to stay visible, and most already film more than they can edit. A beach shoot, a restaurant opening or a client walkthrough can feed weeks of posts if someone has time to cut it.

20CLIPS is a remote editing service for businesses and creators in Sydney. You upload footage to a shared folder and 20 finished videos return each month, for a flat $999 in US dollars. How it works from Sydney

A 30-minute call gets things started: what you film, which references matter and how often footage will arrive. After that you upload to the shared folder and review edits as they return, with 2 revisions each. You can cancel anytime.

Many Sydney owners upload straight after a shoot, whether that is a site visit, a class or a service night, and review the edits when they have a quiet moment. Finished videos are ready to schedule with the rest of your content.
