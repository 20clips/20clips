A business that wants regular short-form video can edit it themselves, hire a freelancer, take on an editor, sign an agency retainer or buy a monthly package. The best fit depends on how many videos you need, how much control you want and how much time you have to manage someone.

Questions for any package supplier

Ask every supplier the same questions and compare the written answers. If you already work with people in other cities, a shared-folder process will feel familiar.

  • What exactly comes with each video?
  • How many videos each month?
  • How many revisions are included?
  • How do I send footage and get files back?
  • What is not covered?
  • What is the minimum term and how do I stop?

Red flags

  • No list of what is included
  • Extras that appear later
  • A long contract up front
  • Promises about reach

The routes side by side

Freelancer

Flexible, but each job needs a brief and quality depends on who is free.

Employee

A dedicated person, with wages, on-costs and equipment. Right for constant video work.

Agency retainer

Often adds strategy and filming, at a higher cost than editing alone.

Monthly package

A fixed fee, a repeatable method and no lock-in. Best for steady output of about 20 videos.
