Compared with other ways of working

Editing your own videos costs nothing in cash and a lot of evenings. Twenty vertical videos can take a whole working week, and the job usually stops when business gets busy.

Employing an editor brings wages, on-costs, equipment and supervision. That is right for daily video work and more than about 20 videos a month needs.

Freelancers charge by the job, which suits a campaign. For a regular flow you brief again and accept variation. Agencies tend to bundle in strategy and filming, which is worth paying for when you need it and costs more than editing by itself. The Australian guide to choosing a package sets out the trade-offs.

Using the fee well

Choose topics before you film. A rough plan makes each batch more useful.

Send footage in batches across the month, so you can use revisions and tweak the style.

Share references early. They answer most style questions before editing begins.
