The alternatives, priced

Creators editing their own work seems free, but quality varies and many are not set up for it.

An employee brings wages, on-costs, equipment and supervision. That suits a team testing creative every day and is more than about 20 videos a month needs.

Freelancers per video suit a one-off campaign. A rolling need means briefing again and living with variation. The package swaps both for one fee, and the Australian hiring guide weighs the options.

Checking the value after three months

Paid UGC is about testing, so measure over a few weeks. Count the openings you tried, note which angle held attention and see how quickly new creator footage turned into usable creative. Weigh that against a fee that did not move.

If it is working, send footage built around the winner. If not, there is no long-term commitment to hold you.
