  • Lead with the reaction

Eight formats and ideas

Each one needs ordinary footage, and each can be edited into a finished video for Australia.

Unboxing

The first reaction to a product, cut to start on the reveal. Fitness studios and wellness businesses in Australia can use this to show how they work.

Problem and solution

A creator describes a problem, then shows the product solving it. Cafes and independent stores in Australia can film this during a normal day and edit it later.

Honest review

A direct opinion with the product in hand. Australian event organisers can adapt this to recap a launch or a gathering.

Routine

A creator shows how they use the product in their day. Many Australian accounts use this format to introduce the people behind the brand.

Before and after

Two states with a simple transition. In Australia this idea suits coaches, trades, retail and service businesses, where a real person and a real result make the video.

Comparison

Two options side by side with a clear verdict. An Australian business can film this on a job or in a studio and send the footage straight to the shared folder.

Testimonial

A short spoken endorsement with captions. Australian owners often have this footage already, because it is what they film on an ordinary working day.

How I use it

Step by step usage with callouts. This works well for personal brands in Australia who want their expertise to be visible.
