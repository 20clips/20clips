Be clear about volume and control

Before you contact an editor, decide how many Reels you want each month and how closely you want to steer the look. A Sydney cafe posting several times a week and a regional coach posting weekly will pick different routes. Once the number is fixed the choice usually follows.

What a good Reels editor does

Frames for the interface

Questions to ask before you say yes

Put identical questions to each option and compare answers in writing. If you already work with suppliers in other cities, a shared-folder method will feel familiar.

  • How do you frame Reels for the Instagram interface?
  • How do you style and maintain captions?
  • How many Reels will I get each month?
  • How do I send clips and give feedback?
  • How many revisions are included?
  • Who is responsible for trending audio?

Red flags

  • Reels that look like shrunk landscape video
  • Captions hidden behind the buttons
  • Revisions that cost extra
  • Promises of followers or sales

The three routes, compared

Freelancer

Fine for a single campaign. Every new job means a new brief, and the look can drift.

Employee

Worth it when Reels are one part of a bigger social role. In Australia that means wages, on-costs and equipment.

Monthly service

Suited to a steady feed: 20 Reels for a fixed fee, 2 revisions each and no long-term commitment.
