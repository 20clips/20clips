Red flags to avoid

Tiny subjects and wasted space are a sign of a rushed crop. Contract terms matter, and an Australian business should not be locked in before seeing the work.

Captions that cover the interface

No revisions included

Nobody controls how Instagram distributes a Reel. An Australian business should expect to see the process spelled out before any money changes hands.
