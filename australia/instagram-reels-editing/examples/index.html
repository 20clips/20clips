Reels from an ordinary week

Good Reels rarely come from elaborate shoots. A tradie finishing a job, a barista making the first coffee, a trainer running a session, a shop packing orders: these all give you a clear point in a few seconds. The edit's job is to find it and drop everything else.

The eight ideas below suit ordinary footage and can be turned into finished Reels through Instagram Reels editing in Australia.

Openings and styles

Pick the first frame before you film and you will know which shot to capture. If you are not sure, upload the whole session and ask the editor to suggest a start.

  • Open on the finished result
  • Ask the question people really ask
  • Start mid-action, not with hello
  • Lead with the strongest line
  • Reveal the odd detail early

Styles to try

  • Talking head with highlighted words
  • B-roll to a beat
  • Step-by-step demo
  • Slow showcase shots

Keeping a steady look across different ideas

Ideas can change from week to week without the feed looking messy, as long as captions, colours and pacing stay the same. That is why you share references and brand files on day one and why they are reused on every Reel.

If an idea clearly works for your audience, say so in your notes and the next batch can lean on it. The editor works from your direction, so the more you tell them about what performs, the better each month gets.

Building a full month

Across a month most accounts rotate through three or four ideas. A fitness studio might run session highlights, one-tip clips and customer lines, all filmed in a couple of visits. Clips go in the shared folder and come back finished with 2 revisions included.
