The difference between a Reel and a cropped clip

Five things worth checking on any finished Reel.

It leaves room for Instagram

The app lays buttons, a caption and your name over the picture. A properly made Reel keeps the face, the product and the text out of those areas.

The first second is the pitch

Open on the result or the question. The same frame often becomes your grid cover, so it needs to look good on its own.

Captions have a style

One typeface, one colour set, one way of highlighting a key word, repeated across every Reel.

The cutting feels like Instagram

Brisk, deliberate cuts and a few light zooms. Heavy effects read as advertising and get scrolled past.

The sound was handled

Voice is levelled and any background noise, such as wind outdoors or a coffee machine, is managed. Music is chosen and licensed by you.

A month, from first call to final file

It begins with a 30-minute call covering your Reels, your look and how often you will film. You agree turnaround and a delivery schedule there, based on your volume.

Then you upload clips to a shared folder whenever you have them. It is common for Australian clients to send a batch after each shoot instead of leaving it all until the end. Finished Reels come back for review, and each has 2 revisions included so a different opening or a corrected caption is a note and not a new job.

Filming tips that make editing easier

If you can only improve one thing, make it audio. A cheap clip-on microphone changes outdoor and workshop footage more than any camera upgrade.

  • Clips at the best quality your phone allows
  • Longer sessions, such as a whole class, that can become several Reels
  • A few Reels whose feel you like
  • Fonts, colours and logo
  • Anything you must say or show

Things that slow a Reel down

  • Filming sideways out of habit
  • A long hello before the point
  • Captions that are small or too low
  • Switching the look every post
  • Ignoring how the first frame looks as a cover

Employee, freelancer or subscription

Cutting Reels in the app is quick once and tedious twenty times. An employee brings wages, on-costs, equipment and supervision, and for roughly 20 Reels a month that is rarely the efficient answer. Freelancers suit a single campaign, though each new job means starting the brief again.
