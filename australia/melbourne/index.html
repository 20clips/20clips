A city with plenty to film

Melbourne is known for its food, coffee, fashion, music, sport and creative scene, and its businesses and creators have plenty to film. Cafes and bars, boutiques, studios, galleries, gyms, agencies and personal brands all produce footage that could work as short-form video if there were more hours in the week.

20CLIPS is a remote editing service that works with businesses and creators in Melbourne. The service is remote, so it works wherever in Melbourne you film. A cafe owner, a studio and a marketing team all follow the same steps: upload, review, revise, publish. The national view is on the Australia hub.

How it works from Melbourne

A 30-minute call sets it up: what you make, which references to follow and when footage will arrive. From there, footage goes into the shared folder and edits come back for your review, each with 2 revisions. You can cancel anytime.

Venues often upload the morning after a busy night, while studios batch weekly. Finished videos are ready to schedule whenever suits your calendar.
