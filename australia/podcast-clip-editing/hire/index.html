Good clips need a listener

Clipping a podcast well means listening for the moment that stands alone, tightening speech so it flows and framing people in a portrait shot. Before you contact anyone, decide how many clips you want each month and whether you record video.

Australian shows usually weigh a freelancer, a producer on the payroll and a package. For the price of the package read the cost page, and for what a month contains read the service page.

What to look for

An ear for moments

Ask how the editor chooses clips from a full recording.

Speaker framing

Cutting between speakers in portrait is a skill.

Accurate captions

Names, terms and timing should be right.

Show branding

Clips should feel like the same show.

Reliable volume

A steady stream of clips keeps episodes visible.

A short commitment

A trial month beats a long contract.

Questions to ask

Put the same list to every candidate and keep the written answers. If you already work with people in other cities, a shared-folder method will feel familiar.

  • How do you choose clips?
  • How do you frame several speakers?
  • How are captions styled?
  • How many clips each month?
  • How do revisions work?
  • Can I pick timestamps?

Red flags

  • Clips with no context
  • Rambling clips
  • Misspelt names
  • Promises about listeners

The routes compared

Freelancer

Good for one episode. A weekly show means a new brief each time.

Producer

Good for a full team, with wages, on-costs and equipment attached.

Monthly package

Suits a weekly show: a flat $999 buys 20 clips, the process repeats and you are free to leave.

Think in monthly totals

A low per-clip quote can conceal charges for captions, framing and revisions. Add up a month at your volume and compare totals.

If you record several episodes in a busy week, ask how each supplier handles it. A fixed monthly volume spread across the weeks is usually simpler than paying per clip.

The first batch is the test

Check that every clip stands alone, that names are right, that the framing follows the speaker and that the look suits your show.

Should anything miss, share a few clips from shows you rate and explain what you like about them. Direction lifts quality faster than anything else.
