The price for Australian shows

Podcast clip editing with 20CLIPS costs $999 a month in US dollars for 20 clips, about $50 each. The fee covers choosing moments, framing speakers, captions and subtitles, sound design and music, basic motion graphics, 9:16 delivery and 2 revisions per clip.

A host or producer in Australia can budget for a month of clips with a single figure. It does not move with episode length or the number of guests. The service page explains the work, and the pricing page lists the inclusions.

What changes a clip editing bill

Episodes recorded

More episodes means more raw material while the price stays at 20 clips.

Video recordings give stronger clips. Audio-only shows can use visuals and captions.

Speakers

Cutting between several speakers takes care. It is part of the work.

Caption accuracy

Names and terms need to be right, so send guest names.

Revisions

Two rounds on every clip.

The alternatives, compared

Clipping your own show costs only time, and time is what a busy host is short of.

A producer on the payroll brings wages, on-costs, equipment and supervision. That suits a full team and is heavy for about 20 clips a month.

Freelancers per episode are flexible, but each episode needs a new brief and the look can drift. A package replaces both with one fee, and the Australian hiring guide sets the three routes side by side.

Stretching 20 clips further

Record video if you can. Clips with faces perform better.

Always send guest names so captions are accurate.

Mark favourite moments. They give the editor a head start.

Not included

  • Recording or producing the show
  • Show notes or full-episode editing
  • Publishing the clips
  • A track you license yourself
  • More than 20 clips a month

Terms

The monthly fee never changes, there is no setup charge and no long-term commitment, so you can stop whenever you like. If your show needs more than 20 clips, mention it on the 30-minute call.

A sense check for smaller shows

Add up the hours twenty clips would take to find, cut, frame and caption. For most hosts it is more than a working week, and it comes out of time that would go on booking guests and recording.

If the show is new or irregular, a lighter plan may fit better, and the 30-minute call is the right place to say so before you commit.
