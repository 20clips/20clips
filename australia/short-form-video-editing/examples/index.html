Start with what you already film

The best short-form ideas usually come from everyday work: finishing a job, running a class, plating a dish, answering a client question for the fifth time that week. Australian businesses film these things constantly. What turns them into content is one clear point and an edit that removes everything else.

The eight formats below work with ordinary footage. Each can be made into a finished vertical video through short-form editing in Australia, with captions, sound and graphics added.

Eight formats that work

Job before and after

Show the problem, the work and the result in three quick cuts. Ideal for trades, landscaping, cleaning and renovation.

The common mistake

Name a mistake you see often, then show the correct way. The first line is the hook.

Class highlight

A few seconds of energy from a class, session or workshop, with captions naming what is being done.

Meet the owner

A short introduction to the person behind the business, with b-roll of the work.

Straight answer

One customer question, answered clearly to camera in under a minute.

A week in the shop

A fast sequence of the week's work, from deliveries to customers.

Behind the product

How something is made or prepared, shot close and cut to music.

Local event

A short recap from a market, launch or community day.

Openings and building blocks

Choose the opening before filming and you will know which shots to capture. When you cannot decide, upload the full recording and let the editor propose the strongest start.

  • Show the finished result first
  • Ask the question customers ask
  • Start mid-job, not with a greeting
  • Open with the boldest line in the recording
  • Reveal the surprising detail early

Common structures

  • To camera, cut for rhythm
  • Voice over on footage of the work
  • Screen recording with zoom
  • Fast montage set to a beat

From ideas to a full month

Across a month most accounts rotate three or four formats. A trades business might alternate before-and-afters, mistakes and meet-the-owner clips from two or three site visits. Everything goes into the shared folder and returns captioned and finished, with 2 revisions included.

It helps, but not strictly. Send footage and references, and the editor finds strong moments, though you set the message.
