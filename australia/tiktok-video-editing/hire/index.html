Define the account before you look for an editor

Write down how many TikToks you want each month, how you would like the account to sound and whether you also run paid TikTok. A Melbourne cafe and a regional trades business will answer those differently, and the answers steer the choice of route.

What to look for

Retention instincts

Ask how they decide what to cut and what to keep to hold attention.

A feed-native look

Overproduced videos stand out for the wrong reasons. Ask for work that looks at home on TikTok.

Caption placement

Text has to sit where TikTok's interface will not cover it.

Audio craft

Poor voice levels and music choices stand out on a sound-on platform.

Questions to ask every candidate

Compare written answers rather than sales calls. If you already work with suppliers in other cities, a shared-folder workflow will feel familiar.

  • How do you build a hook and a payoff?
  • Where do captions sit in TikTok's interface?
  • How many videos will I receive each month?
  • How do revisions work?
  • Can the same edit be adapted for Reels and Shorts?
  • Who handles audio licensing?

Red flags

  • Slick but lifeless edits
  • No clear answer on audio licensing
  • One house style for every client
  • Promises of growth

The three routes at a glance

Freelancer

Good for a campaign. A continuous feed means new briefs and uneven style.

Employee

Good when video is a daily job. It brings wages, on-costs and equipment.

Monthly service

Good for a steady feed: 20 videos for $999, a repeatable process and no lock-in.

What a sensible budget looks like

Compare suppliers on the total for the month, not the headline per-video rate. A low price that adds charges for captions, music and each revision can finish above a flat monthly fee, and a supplier you have to rebrief each time costs hours that never appear on an invoice.

For a small business the fixed fee is also easier to explain to a bookkeeper: one line, the same each month, cancellable without penalty.

Treat the first batch as an audition

Check how fast the first videos open, whether captions clear the interface, whether the audio is clean and whether a requested change comes back as asked. Four yeses suggest the standard will hold.

If the first batch feels generic, send two or three TikToks you wish were yours. Direction is the fastest way to lift quality, and it works the same for a business in a capital city or a regional town.
