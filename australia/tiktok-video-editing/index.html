How to tell a TikTok has been edited properly

Five checks.

Each cut earns the next second

Filler is trimmed, pauses are tightened and the picture refreshes at the right moments.

There is a shape to it

Hook, build, payoff. If a recording does not have one, the editor finds it, often by reordering.

Captions sit in the safe area

TikTok draws its own interface over the bottom and right of the screen. Captions stay clear of it and follow the speech.

Audio is intentional

Clean voice, a fitting music bed and a few effects. Trending sounds are yours to choose and license.

It feels like TikTok

Looser than an advert but not careless: accurate captions, clean cuts, a tidy frame.

How a month works

Start with a 30-minute call about your account, the kind of TikTok you want more of and how often you will film. Turnaround and your delivery schedule are agreed there.

Then upload footage to a shared folder whenever you have it. Many Australian clients send a batch after each filming day, whether that is a shoot in the shop, a recorded class or a day on a job site. Videos come back for review with 2 revisions each, so a different hook or caption is a note, not a restart.

Filming for a better edit

Wind is the enemy of outdoor TikToks. A small clip-on microphone, or even turning your back to the wind, makes voice recordings far easier to edit.

  • Raw clips, including the rough parts
  • Longer recordings such as classes, talks or livestreams
  • TikToks you want yours to feel like
  • Brand fonts, colours and logo
  • Lines or claims that must stay exactly as said

What costs viewers

  • A long hello
  • Overloading a casual clip with effects
  • Posting one cut everywhere unchanged
  • Wind or room noise on the voice
  • Not looking at what held attention

Employee, freelancer or monthly service
