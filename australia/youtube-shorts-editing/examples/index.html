Long recordings hold Shorts that nobody has cut yet. A coaching session has a clear piece of advice, a travel video has a standout scene and a workshop has one memorable demonstration. The editor finds them, and you decide which topics matter.

Openings and sources

Watch your own long videos and mark moments you would send to a mate. Those are your Shorts, and timestamps give the editor a head start.

  • Lead with the conclusion
  • Open on the funniest or most surprising line
  • Ask the question the video answers
  • Show the result first
  • Start with a bold claim you can support
