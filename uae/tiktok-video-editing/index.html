What a good TikTok edit looks like

Five qualities to look for.

Retention is the aim of every cut

Filler is removed, pauses are tightened and the picture keeps changing at the right moments. Each cut gives the viewer a reason to stay for the next beat.

Hook, build, payoff

A video that holds attention opens with a hook, builds a clear idea and delivers a payoff before the viewer drifts. Editing finds that structure in raw footage and drops what does not serve it.

Captions that fit the interface

TikTok covers the lower part and right edge of the screen. Captions are placed where they can be read, styled to your brand and timed to the voice.

Sound-on thinking

TikTok is sound-first more than most platforms. Voice clarity, music and effects shape how native the video feels. Trending audio is left to you for licensing reasons.

Casual, never careless

The style is looser than an advert, but still edited. Clean cuts, correct captions and a tidy frame separate intentional from sloppy.

Preparing your footage

Clean voice recording is the cheapest improvement available. A phone held close in a quiet corner beats a better camera in a loud room, and the editor cannot fully fix distorted audio.

What drags a video down

  • A long intro before the point
  • Over-editing a casual style
  • Posting the same cut everywhere unchanged
  • Poor voice recording
  • Never reviewing which videos held attention
