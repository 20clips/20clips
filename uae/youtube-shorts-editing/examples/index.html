Eight formats and ideas

Each one needs ordinary footage, and each can be edited into a finished video for the UAE.

The best minute of the episode

The single strongest self-contained moment of a longer video. This works well for personal brands in the UAE who want their expertise to be visible.

A quick lesson

One lesson extracted from a tutorial, with captions. Clinics, consultants and service firms in the UAE can use this to show how they work.

The punchline

A funny or surprising moment with just enough set-up. Restaurants and cafes in the UAE can film this during service and edit it later.

A hot take

A bold opinion from a conversation, cut tight. UAE event organisers can adapt this to recap a launch or a showcase.

A definition

A plain explanation of a term your audience searches for. Many UAE accounts use this format to introduce people behind the brand.

Before the full video

A teaser from a longer video that makes people watch it. In the UAE this idea suits property, hospitality and retail, where a place or a product can speak for itself.

A story in sixty seconds

A short story from a talk or interview. A Dubai or Abu Dhabi business can film this on location and send the footage straight to the shared folder.

A question answered

A question from the audience and your answer. UAE founders often have this footage already, because it is what they film on a normal working day.
