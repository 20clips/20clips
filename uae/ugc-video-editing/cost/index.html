Comparing the routes

Asking creators to edit their own footage seems free, but the quality and the look vary, and many creators are not set up to edit.

An in-house performance editor is justified by constant creative testing. For a UAE employer it includes salary, visa, equipment and management, and for about 20 videos a month it may be more than needed.

Per-video freelancers suit a campaign. For a monthly flow you brief again each time and accept different results. A package fixes the fee and the process, and the UAE hiring guide compares the options.

Stretching the fee

Brief the creators clearly. A clear brief produces footage that needs less rescue.

Send the raw files. Originals give better results than compressed copies.

Plan hooks in advance. Decide which angles to test and tell the editor.

Not included

  • Finding or managing creators
  • Writing creator briefs
  • Running or managing the ads
  • Music you choose and license yourself
  • More than 20 videos on the standard package

Terms in brief

Judging value across a quarter

Hook testing is the point of paid UGC, so measure over a few weeks. Count how many distinct hooks you tested, which angle held attention and how fast new creator footage became usable creative. Compare the result to a monthly fee that stayed the same.

If it is working, send more footage built around the winning angle. If not, a month-to-month arrangement lets you change course.
