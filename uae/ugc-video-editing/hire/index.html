UGC needs an editor who keeps it real

UGC works when it looks like a person. An editor who polishes too much strips out the quality that made the clip believable, and one who does too little leaves audio problems and long openings. Before you hire, decide how many videos you need and where they will run.

UAE brands generally choose between a freelancer, an in-house editor and a package service. The cost page explains the package price, and the service page explains what a month includes.

Six qualities to test

Keeps the footage honest

The edit should clean up audio and framing without removing what makes it real.

Hook variation skill

Ask how many different openings the editor can build from one clip.

Product timing

Overlays and shots should land when the creator mentions the product.

Ad-ready formats

Videos should upload straight to an ad platform.

Handles several creators

A consistent look across different creators is a skill.

Clear scope and terms

Know what is included and how to stop.

Questions to ask

Ask each candidate the same list and compare written answers. A shared-folder method suits UAE teams used to remote suppliers.

  • How do you keep UGC looking authentic?
  • How are hook variations delivered?
  • How do you place product overlays?
  • How do I send footage from several creators?
  • How are revisions handled?
  • Which formats do you deliver?

Warning signs

  • Over-polished UGC
  • No hook variations
  • Promises about ad results
  • Unclear scope

Why total cost beats the headline rate

A low per-video rate can hide charges for hook variations, overlays and revisions. Add up a month at the volume you plan, including everything you would need to ask for, and compare totals.

For a team briefing several creators, ask how the supplier keeps one look across different people. Consistency is harder than any single edit.

Use the first batch to judge

Check that the clips still feel like a person, that hooks are genuinely different, that overlays land on the right words and that files upload cleanly. Those four checks say more than a sales call.

If the first batch misses, send two or three ads you wish were yours and say what you liked about them.
