The fee for UAE podcasters

Podcast clip editing with 20CLIPS is $999 a month in US dollars for 20 clips, about $50 each. The fee covers choosing the moments, speaker framing, captions and subtitles, sound design and music, basic motion graphics, 9:16 delivery and 2 revisions per clip.

That lets a UAE show or network set a clip budget with one line. The price does not move with the length of an episode or the number of speakers. The work is described on the service page, and the inclusions are on the pricing page.

Comparing the options

Clipping episodes yourself costs only time, which is usually the scarce thing for a host who also records, books and promotes the show.

A producer on the payroll brings salary, visa, equipment and management. That is right for a full production team and too much for about 20 clips a month.

Freelancers per episode are flexible, though each episode needs a new brief and the style can drift. A package fixes both the fee and the method, and the UAE hiring guide sets the three routes side by side.

Getting more from 20 clips

Record video. Clips with faces are more engaging.

Send guest names. Correct captions are a mark of quality.

Flag your best moments. Timestamps give the editor a head start.

Not included

  • Recording or producing the podcast
  • Show notes or full-episode editing
  • Publishing the clips
  • Music you choose and license yourself
  • More than 20 clips on the standard package

Terms

Each month costs the same, there is no setup fee and nothing ties you in, so cancelling is always possible. If a show needs more than 20 clips, bring it up on the 30-minute call.

Judging value after a quarter

Clips are a discovery tool, so look at the trend over a few months: how many people reach the show through clips, which episodes drive that and how the clip style has settled. Compare it with a monthly fee that never moved.

If clips are bringing new listeners, send more recordings on the topics that work. If not, you can stop without a penalty.
