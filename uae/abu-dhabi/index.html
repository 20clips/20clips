Short-form video for the capital's mix of businesses

Abu Dhabi has its own mix of organisations that rely on video: developers and property firms, hospitality groups, healthcare providers, education and training brands, corporate teams, event organisers and personal brands. Many film regularly, whether that is a venue tour, a conference session or a customer story, and find the footage sits unused because nobody has the time to edit it.

20CLIPS is a remote editing service that works with businesses and creators in Abu Dhabi. Upload raw footage to a shared folder and receive 20 ready-to-publish short-form videos every month for a flat $999 in US dollars. Each one is formatted for Reels, TikTok and YouTube Shorts and arrives with captions, sound design and motion graphics.

Editing happens remotely, so it makes no difference whether you film on Yas, in the city centre or elsewhere in the emirate. The process is the same for a single founder and for a marketing team. The country-wide view is on the UAE hub.

How it works from Abu Dhabi

A 30-minute call comes first, where you agree what you film, which references to follow and your delivery schedule. Raw footage goes into the shared folder, and edited videos return for review with 2 revisions each. You can cancel anytime.

Event teams often upload a full session recording the day after, and training providers upload each webinar as it ends. Finished clips are ready for you to publish whenever it suits your calendar.
