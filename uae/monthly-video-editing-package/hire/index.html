Four ways to get short-form video made

A UAE business that wants regular short-form video has four realistic options. You can edit yourself, hire a freelancer, employ an editor or buy a monthly package. An agency retainer is a fifth, though it often includes strategy and filming. The right choice depends on volume, control and how much management time you can spare.

Questions to ask any package provider

Put the same questions to each provider and compare the written answers. A shared-folder workflow is easy for UAE teams that already work with remote suppliers.

  • What exactly is included in each video?
  • How many videos do I receive each month?
  • How many revisions does each video include?
  • How do I send footage and receive files?
  • What is not covered?
  • What is the minimum term and how do I cancel?

Warning signs

  • No list of inclusions
  • Add-ons that appear later
  • A long contract up front
  • Promises about reach or sales

The routes compared in the UAE

Freelancer

Flexible, but each job needs briefing and the quality depends on who is free.

In-house editor

Embedded in the business, with salary, visa and equipment. Worth it for constant video work.

Agency retainer

Often includes strategy and filming as well as editing, at a higher cost than editing alone.

Monthly package

A fixed price, a repeatable process and no lock-in. Best for steady output of around 20 videos.
