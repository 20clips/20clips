Video editing for a city that films everything

Dubai is one of the most competitive places to be visible on social media. Property developers, hotels, restaurants, clinics, boutiques, consultants and personal brands all publish into the same feeds, and the ones that post consistently tend to be remembered. Most of them already film the raw material: a showroom walkthrough, a chef at the pass, a founder explaining an offer, a launch night. What is missing is the time to turn it into finished, branded short-form video.

20CLIPS is a remote editing service for businesses and creators in Dubai. You send raw footage to a shared folder, and 20 ready-to-publish videos come back every month for a flat $999 in US dollars. They are formatted for Instagram Reels, TikTok and YouTube Shorts, with captions, sound design and motion graphics included.

Nothing depends on where in the city you film. A team in a Downtown office, a studio in a business district and a creator working from home all use the same process: upload, review, revise, publish. The wider picture for the country is on the UAE hub.

How it works from Dubai

Start with a 30-minute call. You say what you make, show the videos you admire, hand over your brand guidelines and agree when footage will arrive. Footage then goes into the shared folder whenever you have it, and edited videos return for review with 2 revisions each. There is no long-term commitment.

Many Dubai clients upload after each shoot, a new menu on Monday and a showroom visit on Thursday, which spreads the editing across the month. Approved videos are finished files that you post on your own schedule.

A sample month for a Dubai business

An illustration of the rhythm, not a forecast.

Week one

Call, references and a first shoot. Settle the caption style on the first videos.

Week two

Send a longer recording with several moments, such as a founder session or a full venue tour.

Week three

Film around what worked, then upload it. Try a different opening on one video.

Week four

Send the last batch to use all 20 videos and plan next month. Unused videos do not roll over.
