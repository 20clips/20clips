What to look for

Six qualities that separate a good short-form video editing provider from a risky one.

A clear process, not just a portfolio

A strong reel shows taste. A clear process shows you will get the same quality in month three. Ask how footage is sent, how feedback is given and how many revisions are included. A UAE business should expect to see the process written down before any money changes hands.

Captions and sound handled properly

Accurate, readable captions and clean audio are where weak edits show first. Ask whether both are part of every video. Hiring for a company in Dubai or Abu Dhabi usually means weighing speed of setup against long-term cost.

Consistency across many videos

Making one great video is easy. Matching the same look across twenty is the real test. Ask how brand guidelines and references are used. UAE teams that already work with remote suppliers will find a shared-folder process familiar.

Honest scope

A good editor tells you what is not included, such as scripting, filming or posting, instead of leaving it to be discovered later. In the UAE it is common to compare several options, so ask each one the same questions.

Predictable cost

Per-video quotes can drift once revisions and extras are added. A fixed monthly price lets you budget without guessing. A strong fit shows in how clearly an editor explains what they need from a Dubai or Abu Dhabi client.

Easy to leave

Long contracts transfer the risk to you. No long-term commitment means the service has to keep earning your next month. For UAE brands that post across several platforms, ask how one set of footage is adapted for each.

Questions to ask before you hire

  • How is footage shared, and what formats can I send?
  • How many revisions are included on each video?
  • Are captions, music and sound design part of the price?
  • Who matches my brand style, and how?
  • What is not included?
  • Is there a minimum term or a cancellation fee?
  • How are turnaround and delivery agreed?

Red flags to avoid

Warning signs that tend to turn into problems later.

No stated scope

If it is unclear what is included, extras will appear later. Ask a UAE-focused editor to explain how a Dubai or Abu Dhabi account's style would be matched.

A price that changes per video

Variable pricing makes a monthly budget impossible. Contract terms matter, and a UAE business should not feel locked in before seeing the work.

Long lock-in

Being tied in for a long period before you have seen the work is a risk you do not need. A short call is the quickest way for a UAE team to judge whether the working style suits them.

Promises about views or followers

No editor controls how a platform distributes a video. Be cautious of anyone who promises results. UAE clients benefit from a clear list of what is not included, so there are no surprises later.
