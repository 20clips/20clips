How to hire short-form video editing in the UAE: freelancer, in-house or monthly package

A practical guide to hiring short-form video editing in the UAE: what to look for, what to ask, and how a freelancer, an in-house editor and a monthly package compare.

Start with the volume, not the vendor

Before comparing anyone, decide how many videos a month you realistically need and where they will be published. A restaurant group posting daily Stories and three Reels a week has a different need from a consultant who wants ten clips a month from a single recording. The number decides which route makes sense.

In the UAE there are three common routes. A freelancer is brought in per project, an in-house editor joins the payroll, and a package service takes a fixed monthly volume. The cost page puts numbers on the last of those, and the service page explains what each month looks like.

Six things worth checking

A written process

A good portfolio shows taste. A clear process shows you will get the same quality in month three. Ask how footage is sent, how feedback is given and how many revisions are included.

Captions and sound

Weak edits fail first on captions and audio. Ask whether accurate captions, music and sound design are part of every video or priced separately.

Consistency across twenty videos

Making one great video is easy. Matching one look across twenty is the real test. Ask how brand files and reference videos are used.

Honest scope

A trustworthy provider says what is not included, such as scripting, filming and posting, instead of leaving you to discover it.

A price that does not drift

Per-video quotes grow once revisions and extras appear. A fixed monthly price lets a Dubai or Abu Dhabi team budget in advance.

Easy to leave

Long contracts transfer risk to you. No long-term commitment means the service has to earn the next month.

Questions to ask before you commit

Ask the same questions of every option and compare the written answers, not the sales call. A shared-folder workflow, which is how 20CLIPS works, is familiar to most UAE teams that already use remote suppliers.

  • How do I send footage, and what formats can I use?
  • How many revisions does each video include?
  • Are captions, music and sound design part of the price?
  • Who matches my brand style, and how is it kept consistent?
  • What is not included?
  • Is there a minimum term or a cancellation fee?

Warning signs

  • No stated scope, so extras appear later
  • A price that changes per video
  • A long lock-in before you have seen the work
  • Promises about views, followers or leads
  • Portfolio pieces that all look different from each other

How the three routes compare in practice

Freelancer

Best for a one-off campaign or an unusual format. For a steady monthly flow the repeated briefing and shifting style add up.

In-house editor

Best when video is a daily job. In the UAE it means a salary, visa and equipment for work that is often closer to 20 videos a month than to full-time.

Monthly package

Best for steady short-form output. $999 a month for 20 videos, a fixed process, 2 revisions per video and no long-term commitment.

FAQ

Is a monthly package better than a freelancer in the UAE?

For a steady flow of content the package is usually simpler because the price and process stay the same. A freelancer suits a single campaign.

Do I need an in-house editor?

Only if you have constant video work well beyond about 20 videos a month. Otherwise a package is lighter.
