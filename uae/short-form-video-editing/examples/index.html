Ideas that suit footage a UAE business already has

Good short-form video usually begins with something real: a place, a product, a person, a result. A UAE business tends to have these in abundance, often shot in striking settings, so the idea is usually available before the edit begins. The craft is in choosing one point and cutting everything that does not support it.

These eight formats are starting points. Each needs ordinary footage, and each can be edited into a finished vertical video by short-form editing in the UAE.

Eight formats worth trying

The one-point explainer

A person talks to camera for under a minute and makes one point. Remove the pauses, add captions and cut to supporting visuals. A property consultant explaining one clause of a purchase is a good fit.

Behind the scenes

Show a kitchen before service, a showroom before opening or a studio during a shoot, with captions that explain what the viewer is seeing.

Before and after

Two states and one transition. Works for interiors, treatments, detailing and renovations.

A customer story

A short testimony cut to the single most useful sentence, with the speaker's name on screen.

The myth and the fix

State a common mistake, then show the right approach. It gives the hook for free.

Product in use

Open on the product doing its job, name the benefit in a caption, and keep any logo for the end.

Event highlight

A fast reel from a launch, tasting or talk that gives people a reason to attend next time.

The lesson learned

A founder shares one decision and what it taught them, cut tight with captions.

Hooks to open with

Pick the hook before you film and the editing is faster, because the footage you need is obvious. If you are unsure, send the recording and ask the editor to find the moment worth opening on.

  • Open on the result, then show how it happened
  • Start with a question your customers already ask
  • Begin mid-action instead of introducing yourself
  • Put the strongest line first and the set-up second
  • Show the surprising detail before explaining it

Formats to choose from

  • Talking head: direct to camera, cut for rhythm
  • Voice over: narration over supporting footage
  • Screen recording: a walkthrough with zoom and captions
  • Montage: clips cut to a beat

It helps. If you send footage and references the editor can find the strongest moments, but you decide the topics and the point of each video.

Yes. Raw footage, recordings and older long-form videos can all become short-form videos.

As long as the idea needs. Many run under a minute, and you can agree a target range on the onboarding call.

Which format works best for a service business?

The one-point explainer and the customer story are the simplest, because they need only a person and a camera.

Can one recording supply several formats?

Yes. A single long recording can yield an explainer, a lesson and a story, which spreads the 20 videos across a month.
