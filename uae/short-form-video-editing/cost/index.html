What drives the cost of short-form video editing

Five things that decide what you pay, and how the monthly package handles each one in the UAE.

How many videos you need

The biggest driver of any editing bill is volume. Twenty videos a month is roughly five a week, which is a steady posting rhythm for most accounts. A fixed monthly price makes the volume part of the plan instead of a surprise on the invoice. For a UAE business the useful comparison is a fixed monthly line against costs that change from one project to the next.

How much finishing each video needs

A clip with captions, music, sound design, b-roll and motion graphics takes more editing than a trimmed recording. When those are all included in the price, you are not paying separately for each layer. Companies in Dubai and Abu Dhabi that budget by the month usually prefer a number that does not move.

How clean the footage is

Footage with clear audio and steady framing is faster to edit than footage that needs rescuing. Spending a little more care at the filming stage lowers the effort of every video that follows. A UAE marketing manager can put this in a plan once and stop re-quoting every campaign.

How many revision rounds you use

Revisions are where per-video prices quietly grow. Two revisions per video are included, which is enough for most notes if the brief and references were clear at the start. Because the price is in US dollars, a UAE finance team knows the figure without converting from another currency.

Whether the style stays the same

Rebuilding a look every week costs time. Agreeing fonts, colours and caption style once, at onboarding, keeps the 20 videos consistent and the process efficient. Founders in the UAE who are weighing this against a hire should count the time it takes to manage one, as well as the salary.

How to get more from the monthly price

Small habits that make 20 videos go further.

Send footage in batches

Smaller regular uploads spread the work across the month and let you correct the style early, before it repeats across many videos. In busy UAE categories, steady output often matters more than one standout video, so a predictable cost helps.

Share references on day one

A handful of videos you like and your brand files answer most style questions before any editing starts. UAE clients can ask about anything unusual on the 30-minute call before they commit.

Give notes in one round

Collect feedback from everyone involved and send it together. It uses your two revisions well and keeps the next batch moving. Remote delivery means a UAE team pays for editing, not for a desk or equipment.

Use the long recordings

One long recording can supply several of the 20 videos. Sending full recordings instead of pre-cut highlights gets more finished videos from the same filming day. Most UAE accounts find that the first batch shows whether the monthly volume suits them.
