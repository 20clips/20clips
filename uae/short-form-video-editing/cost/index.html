The short answer for a UAE business

Short-form editing with 20CLIPS costs $999 a month, in US dollars, for 20 finished videos. That is roughly $50 each. The figure does not move with the length of a video or the number of captions on it, because the package covers captions and subtitles, b-roll, sound design and music, basic motion graphics, hook and pacing work, 9:16 vertical delivery and 2 revisions per video.

For a company in Dubai or Abu Dhabi the useful comparison is not against a single video price. It is against what a month of consistent posting costs through each route: your own time, a freelancer paid per job, or a salaried editor. The sections below set that out. What actually drives the cost

Volume

Twenty videos is roughly five a week, which is a steady rhythm for most accounts. If your plan is closer to two a week, you will not use the whole package, and unused videos do not roll over. Decide the volume first.

Finishing

A clip with captions, music and motion graphics takes longer than a trimmed recording. Because those layers are all inside the $999, you are not billed for each one.

Footage quality

Clear audio and steady framing shorten the edit. A founder in a quiet studio is cheaper in effort than someone filmed beside a busy road.

Revision rounds

Per-video pricing tends to grow at this point. Two revisions per video are included, which covers most changes if the references were clear.

Consistency

Re-inventing the look each week costs time. Agreeing fonts and caption style once keeps all 20 videos on one system.

Three ways to pay for the same work

Editing it yourself is free in cash and expensive in hours. Twenty vertical videos with captions and sound take a large block of every week, and in a busy sales or operations role it is the first job to slip.

A salaried editor is the other end. For a UAE employer the cost includes the salary, a visa and the usual employment costs, plus a laptop, software and management time. That is sensible for a team with video work every day, and heavy for a business that needs about 20 videos a month.

Paying freelancers per video is flexible, but the price moves with each brief and the style moves with each person. The package is built for the middle: a fixed monthly line, a repeatable process and no lock-in. The hiring guide for the UAE compares all three in more detail.

Getting more from the $999

Send long recordings, not just highlights. One forty-minute conversation can supply several videos, and the editor can pick the best moments.

Collect feedback in one round before sending it. Combined notes use your two revisions well and keep the next batch moving.

Share references on day one. Three videos you like answer most style questions before editing begins, and the whole month then runs without back-and-forth about the look.

What is not included

  • Writing scripts or planning content
  • Filming or producing new footage
  • Posting and scheduling
  • Licensed music you choose yourself
  • More than 20 videos a month on the standard package
