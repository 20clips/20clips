Video editing services in the UAE

The UAE has one of the busiest social media environments in the region. Property developers, hotels, restaurants, clinics, retailers, consultants and personal brands in Dubai and Abu Dhabi are all competing for the same attention, and most of them already film plenty: site walkthroughs, product shots, owner explainers, event highlights and client stories. What is usually missing is the time to turn that footage into finished, consistent videos.

20CLIPS fills that gap as a remote service. You send raw footage to a shared folder, and 20 ready-to-publish short-form videos come back each month for a flat $999, in US dollars. The videos are formatted for Instagram Reels, TikTok and YouTube Shorts, with captions, sound design and motion graphics included, so one batch of footage serves every channel your audience in the UAE uses.

How it works from the UAE

Starting takes one 30-minute call. You describe what you make, share reference videos and brand guidelines and agree a delivery schedule. From then on footage goes into the shared folder whenever you have it, and edited videos come back for review. Every video includes 2 revisions, and there is no long-term commitment.

Because the work is remote, it does not matter whether you film in a Dubai showroom, an Abu Dhabi studio or somewhere else in the country. The process is the same for a founder filming alone and for a marketing team with several shoots a week.

If you are not sure which page fits, start with short-form video editing, which covers the whole package, then move to a narrower page for Reels, TikTok or podcast clips if that is most of what you make.

Pricing, terms and what is included

The price is $999 a month, quoted in US dollars, for 20 videos: roughly $50 each. There are no per-video fees, no add-ons and no setup charge, and you can cancel anytime. If you need more than 20 videos, raise it on the call. See the pricing page for the complete list.

Who uses it across the emirates

Property and development teams use it for walkthroughs and project briefings, hospitality groups for dishes, venues and events, clinics and beauty brands for treatment explainers, and retailers for product features. Personal brands and consultants use it to turn long recordings into a steady rhythm of clips.

Agencies in the UAE use it as extra capacity, and brands working with creators use it to give creator footage a consistent finish. Video editing for brands, video editing for creators and video editing for agencies describe each case.

Freelancer, in-house editor or a package

UAE companies generally weigh three routes. A freelancer is flexible, but each job needs a new brief. An in-house editor brings salary, visa, equipment and management, which pays off only when video work is constant. A monthly package gives a fixed fee, a repeatable process and the freedom to leave.
