What the alternatives cost a UAE team

In-app editing is free and slow. It works for one clip and rarely survives a posting schedule with twenty Reels in it.

A social media editor on the payroll is a real option if Reels are only one task among many. For a UAE employer it also brings a salary, a visa, equipment and management time, and if the actual workload is around 20 Reels a month it may be more than you need.

Freelancers charge per Reel or per project. That suits a campaign. For a monthly feed it means repeating the briefing and living with a style that shifts. The UAE hiring guide weighs each route.

Getting more from 20 Reels

Film vertically where you can. It gives the editor more picture and speeds up every edit.

Send longer sessions as well as short clips. A single recording of a chef, a consultant or a showroom tour can become several Reels.

Keep your look steady. Settled fonts and caption style mean every Reel after the first is faster to build.

Not covered

  • Writing post captions or scripts
  • Filming new clips
  • Scheduling or posting
  • Trending audio licences
  • Beyond 20 videos a month
