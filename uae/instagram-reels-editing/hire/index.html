Work out your Reels volume first

A hotel posting a Reel a day and a consultant posting two a week have different needs, and the right route changes with the number. Settle your monthly volume and your platforms before you start comparing providers.

What to look for in a Reels editor

Reels-native framing

Ask how the editor keeps captions and faces clear of the Instagram interface. It separates a real Reel from a cropped video.

A hook-first approach

The opening decides everything. A good editor can show how footage is reordered so the strongest moment leads.

Caption styling you control

Captions are a visible part of your brand. Ask how fonts, colours and highlights are agreed and kept.

Reliable volume

A Reels account lives on rhythm. Check the monthly number is realistic.

Clear revision rules

Know how many changes are included and how to ask for them.

Short commitment

A brief or no commitment lets you test the style against your account.

Questions to put to every supplier

A shared-folder process is easy for UAE teams that already manage remote suppliers. Compare the written answers from each provider, not just the pitch.

  • How are Reels framed for the Instagram interface?
  • How will captions be styled and kept consistent?
  • How many Reels can you deliver each month?
  • How do I send footage and give feedback?
  • Which revisions are included?
  • Who handles trending audio?

Warning signs

  • Reels cut from wide footage with no reframing
  • Captions that sit under the buttons
  • No revisions in the quote
  • A promise of reach

How the options differ

Freelancer

Right for one campaign. Repeated briefing and drifting style are the cost of using one every month.

In-house editor

Right if Reels are part of a larger role. For a UAE employer it adds salary, visa and equipment.

Monthly service

Right for a steady feed. A fixed price, 20 Reels, 2 revisions each and no lock-in.

What a trial month should tell you

Whichever route you choose, judge it on the first batch. By the end of week one you should know whether the Reels look like your brand, whether the captions are readable on a phone and whether feedback leads to the change you asked for. If those three things are true, the rest is volume.

A trial is also the quickest way to learn your own habits. Many UAE teams discover that their best footage comes from a particular kind of shoot, such as a chef at the pass or a showroom at opening, and then plan the next month around it.
