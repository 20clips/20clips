Hooks to open with

  • Show the finished result in the first second
  • Ask the exact question your customer types into search
  • Start with a surprising close-up
  • Lead with a number or a comparison you can back up
  • Cut to a face reacting before explaining why

Eight formats and ideas

Each one needs ordinary footage, and each can be edited into a finished video for the UAE.

Product close-up

A short sequence that shows texture, use and result, cut to music with captions naming the benefit. A Dubai or Abu Dhabi business can film this on location and send the footage straight to the shared folder.

Day in the life

A fast tour of a working day, with captions and quick cuts that keep it moving. UAE founders often have this footage already, because it is what they film on a normal working day.

Three tips in thirty seconds

A spoken list, each tip appearing on screen with a punchy caption. This works well for personal brands in the UAE who want their expertise to be visible.

Customer reaction

A real reaction to a product or result, edited to start on the reaction. Clinics, consultants and service firms in the UAE can use this to show how they work.

Space tour

A walkthrough of a venue, showroom or property, set to music with room names as captions. Restaurants and cafes in the UAE can film this during service and edit it later.

Transformation

A start state, a simple process, a finish state. Visual and easy to follow. UAE event organisers can adapt this to recap a launch or a showcase.

Q and A reply

Answer a real question from your audience in one cut. Many UAE accounts use this format to introduce people behind the brand.

Team or owner introduction

A person introduces who they are and what they do, with captions and light b-roll. In the UAE this idea suits property, hospitality and retail, where a place or a product can speak for itself.
