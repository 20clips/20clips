What is performance creative editing?

Paid social performance depends on creative volume and variation as much as targeting. 20CLIPS edits your raw UGC and product footage into short-form ad creatives for Meta, TikTok and other paid channels: 20 videos a month for $999.

Each creative is built around a clear hook, a defined angle, pacing that holds attention and a visible call-to-action, with room for hook variations and different angles across the same underlying footage.

FAQ

Do you guarantee better ad performance or ROAS?

No. We edit creative to a high standard, but ad performance depends on many factors outside of editing, including targeting, offer and audience. We don't make performance guarantees.

Can you create multiple hook variations from the same footage?

Yes, testing different hooks and angles from the same underlying footage is a core part of performance creative editing.

Do you run or manage the ad campaigns?

No, we edit the video creative only. Campaign setup, targeting and budget management are handled by you or your media buying team.

What footage do you need to start?

Raw UGC, product footage, founder or spokesperson footage, or existing creative you want reworked into new variations.
