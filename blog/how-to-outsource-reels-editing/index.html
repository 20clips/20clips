How to Outsource Reels Editing

20CLIPS Blog · 6 min read

Outsourcing Reels editing usually happens for one of two reasons: you don't have time to edit consistently, or you've hit a volume where editing yourself no longer makes sense. Either way, the process of setting it up is fairly consistent.

1. Decide how many Reels you actually need

Before choosing who to outsource to, get clear on volume. Posting 3-5 Reels a week is a very different scope than posting daily across multiple accounts. Volume determines whether a single freelancer, a subscription service, or an agency is the right fit.

2. Choose a model

Freelancers work well for occasional or lower-volume needs, but require you to manage briefs, feedback and availability yourself. Subscription-style editing services: a flat monthly volume of videos for a fixed price: tend to work better once you need a consistent, predictable output every month, since there's one workflow instead of several freelancer relationships.

3. Prepare your footage and brand materials

Whoever edits your Reels will need raw footage (phone or camera clips, podcast recordings, UGC), plus a few reference videos and any brand guidelines: fonts, colors, caption style. This onboarding step is what lets an outside editor match your existing feed instead of guessing at your style.

4. Set expectations on revisions and turnaround

Agree upfront on how revisions work (how many rounds, what counts as a revision) and how long turnaround typically takes. This avoids friction later and sets a realistic cadence for how often you can expect finished Reels back.

5. Build a repeatable delivery workflow

The best outsourced editing relationships run on a simple, repeatable loop: you send footage on a schedule (weekly or monthly), the editor works through it, you review and request changes, and finished Reels come back ready to post. Once this loop is running, editing stops being something you have to think about day to day.
