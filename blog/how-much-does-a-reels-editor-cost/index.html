How Much Does a Reels Editor Cost?

20CLIPS Blog · 5 min read

Reels editing pricing generally follows the same patterns as short-form editing in general, with a few Reels-specific factors worth knowing before you budget.

Freelance Reels editors

Per-video rates for freelance Reels editing commonly range from $10-40 for newer editors to $50-150+ for editors with strong hook and pacing skills. Many freelancers also offer monthly retainers for a set number of Reels, typically landing somewhere between $500 and $2,500 a month depending on volume and experience.

What changes the price

  • Experience: editors who understand retention and hooks charge more than those who simply trim and add captions
  • Turnaround: 24-48 hour turnaround typically costs more than a multi-day window
  • Revisions: more included revision rounds raise the effective cost per video
  • Source footage: raw, unscripted footage takes longer to shape than clean, pre-planned takes
  • Extras: custom motion graphics, multiple hook variations, or multi-platform versions all add time

Subscription-based Reels editing

Flat monthly subscription services price around a fixed number of videos per month rather than a per-video rate. This tends to work out cheaper per video at higher volumes, and removes the need to negotiate or invoice separately for each Reel. As an example, 20 Reels a month for $999 works out to roughly $50 per video, with captions, b-roll, sound design and revisions included in that price rather than billed separately.

Which model is worth it?

If you only need a Reel here and there, a freelancer on a per-video basis is simpler. If Reels are a consistent part of your content strategy: multiple per week, every month: a flat monthly rate is usually easier to budget for and scales better as volume grows.
