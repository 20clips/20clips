UGC that earns trust

UGC wins when it looks like a person you could know, then proves its point on screen. A UK brand working with creators can brief them on a handful of formats and edit each return into several versions.

These eight formats suit UGC and can be edited into finished videos through UGC video editing in the UK.

Eight formats to brief

Unboxing

The first reaction to a product, starting on the reveal.

Problem and fix

A creator describes a problem, then shows the product solving it.

Honest review

A frank opinion with the product in hand.

Daily routine

How the creator uses the product in a normal day.

Before and after

Two states and one transition.

Head to head

Two options side by side with a verdict.

Customer story

A short spoken endorsement with captions.

Step by step

How to use it, with callouts.

Hooks and styles

Pick your hooks before the creators film, so they know what to capture and the editor knows what to build.

  • Open on the problem
  • Show the result first
  • Start with a bold opinion
  • Lead with the reaction
  • Name the pain in on-screen text

Styles

  • Selfie, close to the face
  • Demo with hands and product
  • Voice over on footage
  • Split screen
