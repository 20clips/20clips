Openings and sources

Watch your own long videos and note the moments you would forward to a friend. Those are your Shorts, and timestamps give the editor a head start.

  • Give the conclusion first
  • Start on the funniest or most surprising line
  • Ask the question the video answers
  • Show the result, then the reasoning
  • Open with a bold claim you can back up
