Be clear about the source and the volume

Turning long videos into Shorts depends on what you have recorded and how many Shorts you want. A Leeds training firm with weekly webinars and a London creator with a large back catalogue need different volumes. Settle the number first, then compare providers.

What to look for

A way of choosing moments

Ask how the editor finds a section that makes sense without the rest of the video.

Real reframing

A wide shot should be reframed around the speaker, not just cropped.

Strong openings

The first seconds decide a Short, so ask how hooks are built.

Accurate captions

Captions should follow the speech and be easy to read.

Steady monthly volume

Shorts reward regular posting.

Short commitment

Questions to ask

Put the same questions to every supplier and compare their written answers. A shared-folder system will feel normal to UK teams used to remote contractors.

  • How do you choose moments from a long video?
  • How is a horizontal source reframed?
  • How are captions styled?
  • How many Shorts are included each month?
  • How do revisions work?
  • Can the same edit be adapted for other platforms?

Red flags

  • Clips that make no sense without context
  • Wide videos squeezed into vertical
  • No captions
  • Claims about subscribers

Three routes compared

Freelancer

Good for a test batch. A regular channel means rebriefing and uneven style.

In-house editor

Good if video is a daily job. It brings salary, employer costs and equipment.

Monthly package

Compare on total cost

A low per-clip quote can hide charges for captions, reframing and revisions. Add up what the month would cost for the volume you want and compare that figure.

Also think about capacity. If you often record a lot in one burst, such as after a conference, check how each supplier copes. A fixed monthly volume spread across the weeks tends to be simpler than a pay-per-clip arrangement.

Treat the first batch as a test

Check that each Short makes sense alone, that the opening is strong, that the reframing looks natural and that captions are accurate. Those four checks say more than a sales call.

If something is off, send two or three Shorts you admire and say why. Direction is the quickest route to better work.
