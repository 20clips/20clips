What changes the cost of Reels editing

Reels per month

Vertical, well-lit clips with clear sound are faster to cut.

How the other options add up

In-app editing costs nothing and takes a lot of time, which is why feeds that start with good intentions often go quiet in week three.

A UK social editor on the payroll means salary, employer costs, equipment and software. That is sensible if Reels are a small part of a larger role, but not obviously so for a plan of about 20 a month.

Freelancers are flexible. For a continuous feed you pay again in re-briefing time and in style drift. A package turns the monthly cost into one predictable fee, and the UK hiring guide compares all three.

Practical ways to stretch the fee

Film vertically where you can. It gives the editor a full frame to work with.

Send full sessions as well as short clips. One long recording can feed several Reels.

Keep the look steady. Once fonts and caption style are agreed, each Reel is quicker to build.

Outside the package

  • Writing the post captions or scripts
  • Filming the clips
  • Posting and scheduling
  • Licensing trending audio
  • Anything beyond 20 videos a month
