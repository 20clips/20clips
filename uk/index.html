Video editing services in the UK

Short-form video has become routine for UK marketing, whether the goal is a product launch, a customer story, an expert explainer or a look behind the scenes. E-commerce brands, agencies, professional firms, independent shops and personal brands from London to Manchester and beyond all need a steady supply of vertical video, and most already hold the footage. The bottleneck is almost always editing time.

20CLIPS removes that bottleneck as a remote service. You send raw footage to a shared folder, and 20 ready-to-publish short-form videos come back every month for a flat $999 in US dollars. They are formatted for Instagram Reels, TikTok and YouTube Shorts and include captions, sound design and motion graphics, so one set of footage can feed every platform a UK audience uses.

How it runs for a UK team

You begin with a 30-minute call about what you make and where it is posted, then share references and brand files and agree a delivery schedule. After that, footage goes into the shared folder when it exists and edited videos return for review, with 2 revisions each and no long-term commitment.

The service is remote, so it works the same for a London agency and a regional retailer. Many UK clients upload straight after each shoot, which spreads the editing across the month and lets style adjustments happen early.

Not sure where to start? Short-form video editing covers the entire package, and the narrower pages help once you know your main platform.

What the $999 includes

The fee is $999 a month in US dollars for 20 videos, about $50 each, with no per-video fees, no add-ons and no setup charge. You can cancel anytime, and if you expect to need more than 20 videos you can raise it on the call. The inclusions are listed on the pricing page.

Who uses it across the UK

Retailers and direct-to-consumer brands use it for product features and creator clips. Agencies use it to add editing capacity without hiring. Professional services firms turn talks and webinars into clips, and independent venues film nights and menus. Creators use it to keep publishing while they film the next project.

Video editing for brands, video editing for agencies and video editing for creators describe how each group works with the package.

Employee, freelancer or monthly service

Hiring a UK editor in-house means salary, employer costs, equipment and management, which only pays off when video is a daily task. A freelancer is flexible but every project starts from a fresh brief. A monthly package offers a fixed fee and a standard process with no lock-in.
