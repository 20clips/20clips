Marks of a TikTok that was edited well

Every cut serves retention

Slack moments are removed, pauses are tightened and the picture refreshes just before attention would drop.

A structure the viewer can feel

Hook, build, payoff. If a clip lacks one, the editor finds it in the footage, often by reordering.

Captions that fit

TikTok covers part of the screen with its own interface, so captions live in the safe area and follow the speech.

Sound with intent

Clean voice, a fitting bed of music and a few well-placed effects. Trending sounds are your choice and your licence.

Tidy but not stiff

TikTok style is looser than an advert, but accurate captions and clean cuts show someone cared.

The process month by month

Everything begins with a 30-minute call. You describe the account, what you want more of and how often footage will arrive, and you agree a delivery schedule that fits.

After that, files go into a shared folder as they are shot. UK clients often upload straight after a shoot, which spreads the editing and gives you early notice if a caption style or a hook pattern is not right. Edited videos return for review with 2 revisions each, so a stronger first line or a different music bed is a note to the editor, not a restart.

Approved videos are ready to schedule. To see how editing fits into a wider content plan, read what makes a good TikTok edit.

What to send

Record voice cleanly if you can. A phone close to the speaker in a quiet corner does more for a TikTok than a better camera in a noisy shop.

  • Raw footage, rough bits included
  • Livestream replays or long recordings to be cut up
  • TikToks you would like yours to resemble
  • Brand fonts, colours and logo
  • Statements that must be kept word for word

What loses viewers

  • A long introduction
  • Effects piled onto a casual clip
  • Reposting one cut everywhere without adjusting
  • Muddy voice recordings
  • Not checking which videos held attention

Employee, freelancer or monthly service

An in-house TikTok editor is valuable if video is a daily task, and in the UK it means salary, employer costs and equipment. Freelancers suit a campaign, though each job starts with a new brief and the quality can vary. Editing in the app suits casual clips and struggles with consistent branded output.
