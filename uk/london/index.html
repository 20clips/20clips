London brands film more than they publish

London is where a large share of UK brands, agencies, start-ups, creators and professional firms compete for attention online. Retailers, restaurants, consultancies, studios and personal brands all know they should be posting short-form video, and most have more footage than time. A product shoot, a founder interview, a talk or a behind-the-scenes day is easy to film and hard to turn into a month of polished videos.

20CLIPS is a remote editing service for businesses and creators in London. Footage goes into a shared folder, and 20 ready-to-publish videos come back every month for a flat $999 in US dollars. Each is formatted for Instagram Reels, TikTok and YouTube Shorts, with captions, sound design and motion graphics included.

Editing is remote, so it works the same wherever in London you film. Agencies use it to add capacity without hiring, in-house teams use it to clear a backlog and creators use it to keep posting. The national picture is on the UK hub.

How it runs for a London team

Book a 30-minute call, share references and brand files and agree a delivery schedule. Footage goes into the shared folder, and edited videos return for review with 2 revisions each. There is no long-term commitment.

Agencies often set up one folder per client and upload as each shoot lands. In-house teams tend to batch weekly. Either way approved videos are finished files you schedule yourself.
