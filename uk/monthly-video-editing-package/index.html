What a package changes about buying editing

Buying video editing project by project is slow. Each job needs a brief, a quote and a decision, and the cost changes with every revision. A monthly package turns that into a standing arrangement: you agree a volume, send footage when you have it and receive finished videos against a fixed fee.

For UK teams the 20CLIPS package is 20 short-form videos a month for $999 in US dollars, roughly $50 per finished video. There are no per-video fees or add-ons, and you can cancel anytime. It is built for creators, brands and agencies that need consistent output without recruiting an editor.

The monthly cycle

A 30-minute call starts the relationship. You say what you make, where it is posted and how often footage will arrive, share references and brand files, and agree a delivery schedule.

After that the cycle repeats. You drop footage into a shared folder, videos come back for review and you request changes within your two revisions. Approved files are ready to publish. UK clients often upload straight after a shoot, which keeps the editing spread over the month and lets style adjustments happen early.

Unused videos do not carry over, so planning shoots across the weeks is the key habit. For help choosing a number, read how many short-form videos to post per week.

Scope: what sits outside

The package edits footage you provide. Fixing the scope is what lets the price stay fixed, and it means there are no surprises about what a month covers.

If you expect to need more than 20 videos, mention it on the 30-minute call and it can be discussed before you commit.

Clear boundaries

  • No scripting or content planning
  • No filming of new footage
  • No posting or scheduling
  • No licensed music of your own choice
  • Standard volume is 20 videos

Package, freelancer, employee or agency

A freelancer is flexible, but each project means a new brief and the result depends on who is free. An in-house editor gives you a dedicated person, with salary, employer costs, equipment and management that only pay off when video work is constant and well beyond 20 videos a month. An agency retainer often adds strategy and filming, which can be exactly right and typically costs more than editing alone.

A package sits between them with a fixed fee, a repeatable method and the freedom to leave. See what a monthly editing package costs in the UK and how to choose a monthly editing package in the UK.
