How the price compares with other routes

Editing in-house is free in cash and costly in hours. Twenty vertical videos take a big share of a working week, so the task tends to be dropped when other things get busy.

A salaried editor brings salary, employer costs, equipment and management. That is the right investment for constant video work and more than about 20 videos a month needs.

Freelancers are paid per job. That suits campaigns, but for a regular flow you rebrief each time and accept some variation. Agencies often bundle strategy and filming, which has value and costs more than editing alone. The UK guide to choosing a package weighs the options.

Practical advice for using the fee well

Decide topics before shoots. A plan makes each batch of footage more valuable.

Upload in batches through the month so you can use revisions and adjust style as you go.

Provide references at the start. They settle style questions early.

Not covered

  • Scripting and planning
  • Filming
  • Posting
  • Music you license yourself
  • More than 20 videos a month
