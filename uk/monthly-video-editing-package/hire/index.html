Questions for any package supplier

Send the same questions to every supplier and compare written answers. A shared-folder process will feel natural to UK teams already using remote contractors.

  • What is included in each video?
  • How many videos do I get each month?
  • How many revisions are included?
  • How do I send footage and receive files?
  • What is not covered?
  • What is the minimum term and how do I leave?

Red flags

  • No list of inclusions
  • Extras that appear later
  • A long lock-in
  • Promises about reach

The routes compared

Freelancer

Flexible, but every job needs a brief and quality depends on availability.

Employee

A dedicated person, with salary, employer costs and equipment. Right for constant video work.

Agency retainer

Often adds strategy and filming, at a higher price than editing alone.

Monthly package

Fixed fee, repeatable method, no lock-in. Best for steady output of about 20 videos.
