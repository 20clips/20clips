Planning the 20

A practical method is to pick four themes and film each in a different format. A retailer might choose product features, customer stories, tips and events, and send footage for one theme per week. Everything goes in the shared folder and returns edited, with 2 revisions on each.

For the price see what a monthly package costs in the UK, and for a buying checklist see how to choose a monthly package. A longer view on rhythm is in how many short-form videos to post each week.

Filling a month from two shoots

You do not need twenty separate shoots. Two well-planned sessions, one in the shop or studio and one with a person to camera, can supply most of a month once the editor starts cutting. The first covers product, process and behind the scenes, the second covers explainers, tips and customer answers.

Add a recorded talk or interview once a month and you will have material for the remaining videos. Plan the questions or shots in advance and the footage practically sorts itself into formats.

One look across the month

A mixed month still looks like one account when captions, fonts, colours and pacing stay the same. References and brand files are gathered at onboarding and reused.
