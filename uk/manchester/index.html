Filming is easy in Manchester, editing is the slow part

Manchester has a strong mix of independent retailers, restaurants and bars, creative studios, media and tech companies, sports and fitness businesses and personal brands. They share a problem: filming is the easy part, and editing a steady supply of vertical videos is what takes the time. A venue night, a product launch or a customer story can fill a week of posts if someone has the hours to cut it.

20CLIPS is a remote editing service that works with businesses and creators in Manchester. Upload raw footage to a shared folder and 20 ready-to-publish videos come back every month for a flat $999 in US dollars, formatted for Reels, TikTok and YouTube Shorts, with captions, sound design and motion graphics included.

Because the editing is remote, it makes no difference where in Greater Manchester you film. The process is the same for an independent venue and for an in-house marketing team. The national view is on the UK hub.

How it works from Manchester

A 30-minute call sets it up: what you film, which references to follow and when footage will arrive. After that you upload to the shared folder and review edits as they come back, with 2 revisions each. You can cancel anytime.

Venues often upload the morning after an event, while shops batch weekly. Either way, finished videos are ready to schedule alongside the rest of your calendar.
