Clips that send people to the episode

The best podcast clips hold one idea that makes sense alone, and make the viewer want the rest. A UK show with a sharp interview style, a good panel or a knowledgeable guest usually has several per episode. The editor finds them; you decide which subjects to put forward.

The eight clip types below can be edited into finished videos through podcast clip editing in the UK.

Eight clip types

A firm opinion

A guest's clear stance, cut to the sentence that states it.

A short story

An anecdote with a beginning and an end.

The laugh

A moment of humour with just enough set-up.

A takeaway

A practical lesson explained simply.

A clash of views

A respectful disagreement.

How they started

A guest's origin story in under a minute.

The rapid answer

One brief reply to a direct question.

The teaser

A moment that makes people want the full show.

Hooks and layouts

Note your favourite moments as you record. A timestamp takes seconds and saves a lot of listening time.

  • Begin on the guest's boldest sentence
  • Open on the question
  • Cut to the laugh, then back up
  • Lead with the conclusion
  • Show the guest's name and credential

Layouts

  • Two shot, both visible
  • Single speaker focus
  • Captions only for audio
  • Waveform with captions
