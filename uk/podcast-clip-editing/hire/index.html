Clips are an editing craft, not a trim

Cutting a good clip from a podcast calls for a listener's ear, a sense of rhythm and the ability to frame several people in a vertical shot. Before you start talking to suppliers, settle how many clips you want each month and whether the show is recorded on video.

UK shows usually weigh a freelancer, a producer on the payroll and a package. The package fee is broken down on the cost page, and the way a month runs is set out on the service page.

What to look for

An ear for moments

Ask how the editor decides which parts of a recording to clip.

Speaker framing

A good editor keeps the person talking in view.

Accurate captions

Names, terms and timing should all be right.

Show branding

Clips should be recognisably from the same show.

Reliable volume

A regular stream of clips keeps episodes in view.

A short commitment

A trial month is better than a long contract.

Questions for each candidate

Put the same list to everyone and keep the answers in writing. Teams that already use remote contractors will find a shared folder familiar.

  • How do you choose clips?
  • How do you frame several speakers?
  • How are captions styled?
  • How many clips each month?
  • How do revisions work?
  • Can I choose moments?

Red flags

  • Clips that lack context
  • Rambling clips
  • Misspelt names
  • Promises about listeners

The routes side by side

Freelancer

Good for a single episode. A weekly show means a new brief every time.

Producer

Good for a publisher with a full team, at the cost of salary, employer costs and equipment.

Look at the whole month

A low price per clip can hide charges for captions, framing and revisions. Add up a month at the volume you plan and compare totals.

If you sometimes record several episodes in one week, ask how each supplier copes. A fixed monthly volume spread across the weeks is usually simpler than pay-per-clip.

Audition with the first batch

Check that each clip stands alone, that names are spelled right, that the framing follows the speaker and that the look matches your show.

If anything is off, send a few clips from shows you like and say why.
