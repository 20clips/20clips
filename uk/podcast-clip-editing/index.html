Anatomy of a clip that works

It needs no context

A good clip is one idea that makes sense without the rest of the episode, whether a story, an opinion or a quick lesson. The editor listens to the full recording to find them.

The speech has been tightened

Conversation contains hesitations and tangents that are fine over an hour and tiresome in forty seconds. Cutting them while keeping the natural rhythm is the difference between a clip and an excerpt.

The frame suits a phone

A wide shot of two people at microphones becomes a vertical clip that cuts between them and keeps whoever is speaking in view.

Captions follow every word

Spoken clips live or die on captions that are accurate, well timed and easy to read.

The show feels like one show

A repeated caption style and palette make clips from different episodes look related.

The month in practice

It starts with a 30-minute call about the show: how many episodes you record, who the audience is and what you want clips to achieve. You agree a delivery schedule there.

You then send your logo, fonts, colours and some clips you admire, and upload each episode to a shared folder after recording. Video gives the best clips, although an audio-only show can use simple visuals with strong captions. Clips return for review with 2 revisions each, and you can mark timestamps you care about.

Approved clips are finished files that you publish. For a step-by-step view of the approach, read how to turn a podcast into short-form content.

Sending episodes the helpful way

If your show is audio-first, consider pointing a camera at the table anyway. The extra reach from clips with faces on them tends to repay the small effort of setting it up.

  • The full recording, with video if you have it
  • Names of the guests and the show
  • Logo, fonts and colours
  • Timestamps you already like
  • Clips from shows you would like yours to resemble

Ways clips go wrong

  • Audio-only files when video exists
  • A punchline with no set-up
  • Clips as long as the topic
  • No guest name on screen
  • Posting clips only on release day
