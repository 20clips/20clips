One monthly fee for UK podcasters

Clip editing for podcasts with 20CLIPS is $999 a month in US dollars for 20 clips, or about $50 each. Choosing the moments, framing the speakers, captions and subtitles, sound design and music, basic motion graphics, 9:16 delivery and 2 revisions per clip are all inside that figure.

A UK producer or publisher can plan a clip budget with one number. It does not rise with the length of an episode or the number of guests. The service page explains the work, and the pricing page lists the inclusions.

The alternatives, costed

Clipping your own show is free in cash and heavy in hours, and hosts usually stop once the novelty fades.

A producer on the payroll brings salary, employer costs, equipment and management. For a publisher with a full team that is natural; for about 20 clips a month it is more than needed.

Freelancers per episode are flexible, but each episode needs a new brief and style tends to drift. A monthly package replaces both with one fee, and the UK hiring guide compares the routes.

Making 20 clips go further

Record video if you can. Faces make clips more engaging.

Always send guest names so captions are right.

Mark your favourite moments. They give the editor a head start.

Outside the fee

  • Recording or producing the show
  • Show notes or whole-episode editing
  • Publishing the clips
  • A track you license yourself
  • More than 20 clips a month

Contract and terms

Every month costs the same and you can leave when you wish, since there is no long-term commitment and no setup fee. If your show needs more than 20 clips, say so on the 30-minute call.

Is it worth it for a smaller show?

Count the hours twenty clips would take you to find, cut, frame and caption. For most hosts that is more than a working week, and it comes out of the time you would spend booking guests and recording.

If your show is brand new or releases rarely, a lighter plan may suit better, and the call is the place to talk about it.
