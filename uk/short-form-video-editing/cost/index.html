Comparing the routes in pounds of effort, not just pounds

Doing it yourself costs nothing in cash and a lot in time. Twenty videos with captions, music and graphics can take most of a working week, and for a UK founder or marketing manager it is usually the first job to slip when other priorities arrive.

An in-house editor brings salary, employer costs, equipment, software and management time. It pays off when video is a daily task. For about 20 videos a month it is usually more than the work needs.

Freelancers are flexible and fair for single projects. For a monthly flow, the repeated briefing and the shifting quality between people add up. A package replaces all of that with a fixed fee and a repeatable process, and the detail is in how to hire short-form editing in the UK.

Where the money goes furthest

Send entire recordings. A webinar or a customer interview supplies several videos from one session, which is the best value for a team that already records things.

Gather feedback internally before sending it. One combined set of notes is easier to action than five separate ones and keeps your two revisions for the changes that matter.

Provide references at the start. They settle questions about style early and prevent a month of small corrections.

Outside the $999

  • Scripts and content planning
  • Filming new footage
  • Posting and scheduling
  • A licensed track you pick yourself
  • Anything beyond 20 videos a month
