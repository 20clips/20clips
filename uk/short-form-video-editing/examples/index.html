Where good ideas come from

The most reliable short-form ideas are not invented in a brainstorm. They come from what a UK business already does every day: packing an order, fitting a customer, running a workshop, answering the same question for the tenth time. Film that, pick one point and edit hard.

Below are eight formats that work with ordinary footage. Any of them can be turned into a finished vertical video by short-form editing in the UK, with captions, sound and graphics added.

Eight formats to try

Answer one question

Take a question customers keep asking and answer it in under a minute, with the question on screen as the hook.

Inside the stockroom

Show how a product is packed, made or prepared. Texture and process hold attention with very little scripting.

Three tips

A spoken list with each tip appearing as a caption. Easy to batch from one recording.

Customer reaction

A real reaction to a product or result, edited to start on the reaction.

The honest comparison

Two options side by side with a clear verdict. Works for services as well as products.

Day in the working life

A fast tour of a day on site, in the studio or behind the counter.

Event recap

A short reel from a launch, talk or open day with captions naming the highlights.

Founder explains

A leader explains one decision in plain language, cut tight with supporting b-roll.

Openings that hold people

Choose the opening before you film, and shooting becomes easier because you know which shots you need. If you cannot decide, send the whole recording and let the editor suggest the strongest start.

  • Start on the answer and explain it afterwards
  • Ask the question people type into search
  • Open mid-action, with no introduction
  • Lead with the sharpest sentence from the recording
  • Show a surprising detail before naming it

Build blocks

  • Talking head, cut for rhythm
  • Voice over on supporting footage
  • Screen recording with zoom
  • Montage cut to a beat

Putting twenty videos together

Most accounts mix formats across a month. A retailer might alternate question answers, stockroom clips and customer reactions across three shoots. Everything goes into the shared folder and returns captioned, mixed and finished, with 2 revisions on each video.

FAQ

Do I need every idea planned?

No. Send footage and references and the editor can find the strongest moments, but you choose the message.

Can you edit older footage into these formats?

Yes. Archive recordings and existing videos can be reworked into short-form videos.

How long should a video be?

As long as the idea needs. Many are under a minute, and you can agree a range at onboarding.

Which format is easiest to start with?

Answering one question to camera. It needs only a person and a phone.

Can one recording make several videos?

Yes. A single long recording often supplies several of the 20 videos.
