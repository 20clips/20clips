FAQ

What makes TikTok editing different from Reels or Shorts?

The core edit is similar, but pacing, caption placement and hook style are adjusted for TikTok's native feel and audience expectations.

Can you edit both organic and paid TikTok content?

Yes. Organic TikToks fall under short-form editing, and paid TikTok ad creatives fall under performance creative editing, included in the same monthly offer.

Do you post the videos for us?

No, we deliver finished, ready-to-publish files. Posting and scheduling stays with you or your social team.

Can I send footage weekly instead of all at once?

Yes, most clients send footage in smaller batches throughout the month rather than one large drop.
