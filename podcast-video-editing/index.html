What is podcast video editing?

FAQ

What format should I send my podcast recording in?

Raw video or audio-plus-video recordings both work. Higher-quality source footage produces stronger clips.

How many Shorts can you get from one episode?

It depends on episode length and content, but a single episode often supplies multiple of your 20 monthly videos.

Can you add captions and visuals to audio-only podcasts?

If there's no video, we can work with static or simple visuals and strong captions, though video recordings produce more engaging results.

Do you choose which moments become clips, or do we?

We identify strong moments based on hooks and pacing, and you can flag specific timestamps you want included.
