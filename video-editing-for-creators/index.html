FAQ

I'm not a full-time creator. Is this still worth it?

If you have footage or a podcast and want to post consistently without editing it yourself, the monthly offer works the same regardless of size.

Can you help me figure out what to film?

We edit the footage you send. We don't script or plan content, though we can flag what's working across your edits over time.

Do I need a professional camera setup?

No, phone footage works fine as long as audio and lighting are reasonably clear.

Can you match a casual editing style rather than a polished corporate look?

Yes, style is set during onboarding based on your brand and reference videos.
