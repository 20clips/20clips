Buyers want the headline first

Property buyers and investors want to understand a project quickly. A short video can brief them on a development, a listing or a market insight in under a minute if it has a clear structure: the headline, the key points and a call to action. Long meeting recordings rarely work as they are.

The example below is a project briefing recorded around a boardroom table and cut into a short, clear summary. It shows the method that applies to any long recording: find the point, say it first and support it.

Who this is for

Developers, brokers, agents and investment firms all record briefings, tours and talks. See Shorts in the UAE, short-form editing in the UK and the monthly package in Australia for 20 videos a month at a flat $999.

What makes real estate edits work

Lead with the headline

State the project or the point first, then support it.

Keep the facts on screen

Key details appear as short captions.

Use the space as proof
