Why browse by niche

Most people who look at editing work are really asking one question: has this studio edited something like mine? A grid of unrelated clips cannot answer that, so the catalogue groups the portfolio by what the videos are about, from cars and real estate to podcasts and food. Each niche page shows the videos, describes what is in each one and explains what makes that kind of edit work.

Every niche page also points to the services and country pages behind it, so you can go from an example to the price, the process and a call in a couple of clicks.

How to use the catalogue

Start with the niche closest to your own content and watch the videos. Then read the short notes under each one: they say what the edit is doing, such as opening on a detail, framing a speaker or cutting to a beat, so you can decide whether that approach suits you.

When something fits, follow the links from the niche page to the matching service and country page, where the price, the process and the answers to common questions are laid out. If nothing fits exactly, describe your footage on the 30-minute call and the editor will say how it would be handled.
