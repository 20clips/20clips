Podcasts short-form editing

A podcast episode holds many short moments, and clips are how people discover a show. A good clip stands alone, starts on the strongest line and uses captions that follow the speech. The edit chooses, tightens and frames each moment for vertical.

These examples are an interview clip and a highlight moment from the 20CLIPS portfolio.

The service behind these edits is described on Podcast Video Editing, YouTube Shorts Editing and Short-Form Video Editing. The price is on the pricing page.

Podcasts content is edited the same way as everything else in the package: footage goes into a shared folder, each video comes back with captions, sound design and motion graphics, and every video includes 2 revisions. A flat $999 a month covers 20 videos, with no long-term commitment, so a podcasts account can plan a full month of content in advance.

What makes podcasts edits work

Choose a moment that stands alone

A clip should make sense without the episode around it.

Start on the strongest line

The hook is often a sentence from the middle of the conversation.

Frame the speakers

Cut between people so the active speaker is always visible.

Style the captions

Captions carry spoken content and carry the show's brand.
