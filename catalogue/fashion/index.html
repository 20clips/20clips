Fashion short-form video combines product, personality and pace. A menswear feature, a campaign reel or a creator wearing the product all need edits that show fit, texture and movement and keep text and captions out of the way of the garments.

What makes fashion and apparel edits work

Show the product in motion

Clothes look best when they move. The edit uses motion, not still shots.

Keep captions light

Text should name the product without hiding it.

Vary the hook

Different openings from the same footage show which angle works.

Make creator footage feel on brand

UGC edits tidy audio and framing while keeping the personality.
