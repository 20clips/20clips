Events short-form editing

Events produce a lot of footage in a short time, and the value fades quickly if it is not published. A good event reel gives people a reason to come next time, a clip for sponsors and content for the weeks after. The edit has to find the highlights, keep the energy and finish fast.

The videos below are events edits from the 20CLIPS portfolio: venue event feature, event venue coverage and event stage set-up reel.

The service behind these edits is described on Short-Form Video Editing, Instagram Reels Video Editing and YouTube Shorts Editing. The price is on the pricing page.

Events content is edited the same way as everything else in the package: footage goes into a shared folder, each video comes back with captions, sound design and motion graphics, and every video includes 2 revisions. A flat $999 a month covers 20 videos, with no long-term commitment, so a events account can plan a full month of content in advance.

What makes events edits work

Pick the moments that carry the room

Crowd reactions, key speakers and standout set pieces make the strongest cuts.

Cut to the music

Pacing a reel to a beat gives it momentum even when the footage is varied.

Add context with text

Event name, place and date on screen make a clip useful when shared.

Make several, not one

A single event can supply many short videos for different platforms.

What to send

  • Raw footage from every camera
  • A list of speakers or key moments
  • Venue and event names
  • Music you are licensed to use
  • Brand assets for sponsors
