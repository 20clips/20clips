FAQ

Do you edit Reels for Instagram specifically, or is it the same as TikTok editing?

The offer covers Reels, TikTok and YouTube Shorts together: most footage gets edited into versions for all three platforms as part of your 20 monthly videos.

Can you match my existing Reels style?

Yes, we onboard with reference videos and brand guidelines so new edits match your existing feed.

Do you handle trending audio and sounds?

We can cut to trending audio you provide or flag suggestions, though sourcing licensed trending audio ultimately sits with you due to platform licensing.

How fast do I get my Reels back?

Turnaround is set on your onboarding call based on your monthly volume and footage delivery schedule.
