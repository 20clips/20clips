What is short-form video editing?

Short-form video is now the main way audiences discover creators and brands, but posting consistently is difficult when editing 20+ vertical videos a month sits on top of everything else you do. 20CLIPS handles the editing every month for a flat $999.

Every video is built around the same fundamentals: a hook that earns attention in the first two seconds, pacing that holds it through the middle, and a clean cut that leaves people wanting the next one. We handle captions, b-roll, sound design and basic motion graphics on every edit, formatted for Reels, TikTok and YouTube Shorts.

Who it's for

  • Content creators publishing daily or weekly across platforms
  • Founders and personal brands turning ideas into content
  • Podcasters who want episodes repurposed into Shorts
  • DTC and e-commerce brands with product or UGC footage
  • Marketing teams and agencies that need reliable monthly output

FAQ

What counts as "footage" I can send you?

Raw camera footage, phone recordings, podcast recordings, screen recordings, UGC clips, or existing long-form video you want repurposed. If you're not sure it will work, ask on a call.

Do you write scripts or hooks from scratch?

We edit and optimize the footage you provide, including trimming and reordering for stronger hooks and pacing. We don't write scripts or produce original footage.

Can I request specific styles or reference videos?

Yes. Most clients share reference videos and brand guidelines during onboarding, and we match that style consistently across all 20 videos.

What if I don't need all 20 videos in a given month?

Unused videos don't roll over, but most clients send footage in batches and use the full 20 each month once their workflow is set up.
