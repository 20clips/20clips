FAQ

Can the same footage be edited for both organic posts and paid ads?

Yes, this is one of the most common workflows: one footage batch, edited into both organic short-form videos and performance creatives.

Do you handle brand guidelines and templates?

Yes, we onboard with your brand guidelines, fonts and colors and apply them consistently across videos.

Can multiple people on our team submit footage?

Yes, we can work with a shared folder that multiple team members or creators contribute to.

Do you shoot video for us?

No, 20CLIPS is an editing service. We work with footage you already have or are sourcing separately.
