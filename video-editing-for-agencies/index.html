What is video editing for agencies?

Agencies need reliable editing capacity that scales without hiring. 20CLIPS works as a white-label short-form editing partner, delivering 20 ready-to-publish videos a month per client for $999.

Editing is delivered under your agency's process: you can present finished work as your own, and add capacity for additional clients as needed without building an in-house editing team.
